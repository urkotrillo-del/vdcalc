Enter the data of a car and a circuit and get its acceleration, braking, top speed, g-g diagram, cornering and lap time.

The first load downloads about 35 MB.

About the model. The car is a point mass with longitudinal and lateral load transfer; suspension dynamics are not modelled. The driver is perfect: the car always uses the grip available, and no more. Each axle carries one type of tyre, with a friction coefficient that may depend on the load. Air density and gravity are constant, and tyre temperature, wear and fuel burn are left out. The circuit is a closed line with height and banking, on a smooth road. The results are estimates meant to compare cars and set-ups, not a substitute for measuring them on track. The calculation runs in your browser and no data leaves it.
